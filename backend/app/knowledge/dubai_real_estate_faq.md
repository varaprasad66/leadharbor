## Golden Visa via property investment

Property worth at least AED 2 million (about USD 545,000) can qualify you for the UAE Golden
Visa — a renewable 10-year residency. The property must be completed, freehold, and registered
with the Dubai Land Department. It covers your spouse and children, and you don't need a local
sponsor. Mortgage properties can also qualify under current rules. Exact conditions change, so
our advisors confirm the latest requirements with the Dubai Land Department before you commit.

## Rental yields in Dubai

Dubai's average gross rental yield is around 6.3-6.5%, well above London or New York. Value
communities outperform: Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) averages roughly 7-8%, Dubai Silicon
Oasis around 8.7%, and International City about 8.6%. Premium areas like Dubai Marina and
Business Bay yield closer to 5-7% but offer stronger capital appreciation.

## Taxes and costs

Dubai charges 0% property tax and 0% personal income tax on rental income. One-off purchase
costs include the 4% Dubai Land Department transfer fee, plus agent and trustee fees (typically
2% + ~AED 4,000-5,000). The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so there's no currency
risk for US investors.

## Freehold ownership for foreigners

Foreign buyers can own property outright (freehold) in designated areas — including Dubai
Marina, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, JVC, Palm Jumeirah, and Dubai Hills Estate. Ownership is
registered with the Dubai Land Department and comes with a title deed. You do not need UAE
residency to buy.

## Payment plans for off-plan property

Developers typically offer payment plans such as 10-20% down payment with installments during
construction, and some projects offer post-handover plans with monthly installments (some as
low as 1% per month). All off-plan payments go into DLD-regulated escrow accounts, which
protects buyers if a project is delayed.

## Mortgages for non-residents

US investors can typically borrow 50-80% loan-to-value depending on the bank, the property and
income documentation. Rates vary by project and profile. Ready properties are easier to finance
than off-plan. We introduce clients to mortgage brokers who specialize in non-resident US
buyers.

## Buying process step by step

1. Choose the property and sign the reservation form with a booking deposit (usually 5-10%).
2. Sign the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA).
3. Pay the Dubai Land Department 4% transfer fee and register the property (Oqood for
   off-plan, title deed for ready).
4. Handover and key collection; for rentals we connect you with leasing and property
   management partners.

## Off-plan vs ready property

Off-plan usually means lower entry prices, developer payment plans and higher capital growth
potential, but you wait for handover (6 months-3 years) and there's construction risk. Ready
property delivers rental income immediately, is easier to mortgage, and can qualify for the
Golden Visa right away. Many investors split: one ready unit for income, one off-plan for
growth.

## Short-term rental and Airbnb

Short-term rentals are legal in Dubai with a DTCM (Dubai Tourism) holiday-homes permit.
Furnished short-term lets in tourist areas often gross 8-12%, above long-term rents, but carry
higher management costs. Professional operators charge around 15-25% of revenue to manage.

## Service charges

Annual service charges (building maintenance) typically run AED 10-30 per square foot depending
on the building and amenities. They are paid by the owner and should be deducted when you
calculate net yield. Our advisors share exact service-charge schedules for every project.

## Best areas for US investors

JVC and Dubai Silicon Oasis for maximum rental yield; Dubai Marina, Downtown and Business Bay
for premium tenants and capital appreciation; Dubai Hills Estate and Arabian Ranches for family
villas. Off-plan hotspots include Dubai South and Waterfront communities near the new airport.

## Rent collection and property management

Most investors use licensed property management companies that handle tenanting, rent
collection and maintenance for roughly 5-10% of annual rent. Rental income can be remitted to
your US bank account without restriction.

## Currency and money transfer

The UAE dirham has been pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725 for decades. Purchase funds usually
move by international wire to a UAE escrow or trust account; amounts above USD 10,000 are
reported on the US side as a standard international transfer.
